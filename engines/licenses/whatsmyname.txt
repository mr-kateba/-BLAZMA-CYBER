Changes made by Blazma Cyber
----------------------------
src/core/username-sites.json is an adaptation generated by scripts/make-username-sites.mjs:
- left out the adult ("NSFW"), dating, political and archive categories, entries marked invalid,
  plain-HTTP checks, checks that need a cookie, and checks whose "missing" answer cannot be told
  apart from "exists";
- kept only the fields needed for the check, renamed them, dropped Host/TE request headers, and
  added a stable id and a "social"/"other" group per site.
The adaptation is distributed under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0).

No warranties are given. Detection rules can go stale when a site changes; Blazma reports any answer
that matches neither rule as "couldn't check" instead of guessing.
